Privacy Policy

App: My Duas  |  Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the “My Duas” mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “our”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not have servers that receive data from the app, and we never see what you enter.

The app does not require an account or login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

To work as a dua counter, the app saves the following information only on your device, in the app’s private local storage:

  • The duas you create (name, target count, current count, chosen icon/colour, optional note)
  • The dates a dua was started and completed
  • Your app settings (for example, whether haptic feedback is turned on)

This data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app.

3. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or any third-party tracking services, so no data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive device permissions. Haptic feedback (a short vibration when you tap the counter) uses a standard Android feature and does not give us access to any data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete a single dua from inside the app
  • Delete all duas from the Settings screen
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its stored data from your device

Because we do not hold any copy of your data, we cannot access, recover or delete it on your behalf.

6. Device backups

If you have Android’s device backup feature turned on, your phone’s operating system may include the app’s local data in your own backup on your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and Google under Google’s privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is a simple counter for personal remembrance and is suitable for people of all ages, including children. The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases, no links to other apps or websites, and no social or chat features, so children can use it safely.

Parents and guardians can review or remove everything the app stores at any time by deleting duas inside the app or uninstalling it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the details below.

8. Security

Your data is stored locally within the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by your device’s own security (such as your screen lock). Since no data leaves the device, there is no data transmission to intercept.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app adds new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
